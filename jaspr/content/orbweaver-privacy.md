title: Orbweaver privacy policy
description: What Orbweaver collects, which is nothing.
---

Last updated 3 September 2026.

**Orbweaver does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or health
information.**

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
network access of any kind. Everything it displays is bundled with the app and
read from your device. It works with the phone in aeroplane mode, which is the
point: it is written for hospitals where the network is not dependable.

## What stays on your device

Small preferences, such as the page you last had open, are stored on the device
so the app can reopen where you left it. They never leave it, and are removed
when you uninstall the app.

## When something does leave your device

Two things can send information out, and both require you to act.

**Links.** Tapping an external link opens your browser or mail app. Whatever you
do there is covered by that app's own policy and by the policy of the site you
visit.

**The "report a problem" button.** This opens your own mail app with a message
already addressed to the developer and pre-filled with which page you were
looking at, the app version, your device's operating system and version, and the
time. You can see and edit all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless
you press send in your mail app. Do not include patient information in that
message. Reports go to greyzus@fhirfli.dev and are used only to fix the app.

## Children

The app is a clinical reference for health workers. It is not directed at
children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
