# Mid-upper arm circumference - Bumblebee — a clinical record for emergency medical teams v0.1.0

## Resource Profile: Mid-upper arm circumference 

 
A tape measure round a child's arm, the standard field test for malnutrition. Taken at first contact and triage, not later in the visit. Recorded as a number in millimetres and never as a yes/no, because the threshold is contested: one long-cited definition says under 110mm, current guidance says 115mm, and screening evidence argues for higher still. Store the measurement and let the deployment set the line.
