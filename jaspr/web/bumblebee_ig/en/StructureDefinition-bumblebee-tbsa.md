# Burn surface area - Bumblebee — a clinical record for emergency medical teams v0.1.0

## Resource Profile: Burn surface area 

 
What percentage of the body is burned. Decides where the patient is treated — above 5% a Type 1 fixed team refers onward, above 20% it needs a Type 3 — and drives fluid resuscitation from the time of arrival.
