# Pain - Bumblebee — a clinical record for emergency medical teams v0.1.0

## Resource Profile: Pain 

 
How much pain the patient is in. The standards require pain to be assessed on arrival AND repeatedly during the stay, so a single score at triage satisfies nothing — which is why the time is mandatory here.
